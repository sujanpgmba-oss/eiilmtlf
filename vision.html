TLF CORE PRINCIPLES

OUR VISION & PILLARS

Envisioning a society where every child receives the opportunity to learn, grow, discover their true abilities, and contribute meaningfully to the world.

1

Mental Strength

Developing mental resilience, self-confidence, emotional stability, determination, and a positive outlook among children living in marginalized communities.

2

Education

Providing foundational and holistic education encompassing the science of life, science of nature, art of work, art of thought, and art of creation, while encouraging curiosity, creativity, and lifelong learning.

3

Unfolding the Inner Spirit

Identifying the inherent abilities and hidden potential within every child and providing opportunities to transform those strengths into meaningful personal and professional pursuits.

4

Economic Empowerment

Supporting children through foundational education, skill development, and vocational exposure so that they can gradually become self-reliant, productive, and responsible contributors to their families and communities.

5

Spiritual and Human Values

Promoting values associated with Sattwa Guna, including truth, righteousness, compassion, self-discipline, and ethical conduct, with the objective of nurturing responsible and value-driven individuals.

6

Ethics and Moral Values

Cultivating discipline, integrity, social responsibility, respect, compassion, and a strong sense of moral responsibility, enabling children to become responsible members of society.
