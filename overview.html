ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

Organizational Overview

Towards Life Foundation (TLF) is a socio-environmental initiative of EIILM-Kolkata that seeks to address the multidimensional challenges faced by children and communities living in conditions of social and economic disadvantage.

Rather than limiting its interventions to short-term relief, TLF emphasizes sustainable social transformation and holistic child development. Its approach integrates education, nutrition, healthcare, hygiene, skill development, environmental awareness, extracurricular activities, and value-based learning.

Through the active participation of faculty members, students, volunteers, professionals, and community stakeholders, TLF works towards creating sustainable opportunities for children and marginalized communities.

OUR CORE COMMITMENTS

Key Areas of Work

A structured framework dedicated to uplifting every aspect of a child's growth and wellbeing.

01

Child Education

TLF provides foundational education in areas such as language, arithmetic, computer literacy, general knowledge, and value-based learning. The initiative aims to strengthen educational foundations while encouraging curiosity, creativity, and confidence.

02

Child Nutrition

The Foundation supports children's nutritional well-being through the provision of nutritious meals, fruits, and other essential dietary support, with the objective of addressing nutritional deficiencies and promoting healthy development.

03

Child Health & Hygiene

TLF organizes health check-up camps conducted by qualified professionals and facilitates the distribution of essential medicines where appropriate. Hygiene awareness and routine health assessments are also incorporated.

04

Skill Development

The Foundation promotes self-reliance through vocational exposure, handicraft activities, practical workshops, and other skill-development initiatives designed to introduce children to productive learning.

05

Extracurricular & Cultural

TLF encourages holistic development through sports training, cultural programmes, art competitions, educational excursions, and recreational activities that promote creativity, teamwork, and confidence.

GUIDING LIGHT

Identity and Philosophy

The identity and philosophy of Towards Life Foundation draw upon Indian philosophical and Vedic traditions, combined with a broader humanistic vision of social responsibility and inclusive development.

Envisioned and guided by Prof. (Dr.) R. P. Banerjee

1. Prana Dipti

The Glowing Vitality

Represents the awakening of inner vitality, hope, confidence, dignity, and the potential for positive transformation within every individual. Helps children recognize their inherent capabilities.

2. Philosophical Motto

«“Let's Wipe Off All Tears from the Dimming Eyes.”»

Connected with the Vedic realization: «“Sarvam Khalu Idam Brahman”» — emphasizing the inherent value, respect, and dignity of every human being.

3. Guiding Principle

Teachings of Swami Vivekananda

«“Daridra Devo Bhava, Murkha Devo Bhava”»

Reflects the importance of serving vulnerable communities with compassion, dignity, responsibility, and a commitment to human development.
