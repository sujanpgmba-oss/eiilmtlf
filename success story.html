SUCCESS STORIES OF TLF

SUCCESS STORY 01
Roshan Sir
01

Every story of change begins somewhere. For Roshan Sir, it began in a place where opportunities were limited and everyday life came with its own struggles.

Growing up in a financially disadvantaged, slum-like community, Roshan Sir experienced from a young age the difficulties faced by children who have potential but lack the resources to move forward. His own journey eventually brought him to Tollygunge Branch, where he studied and received support through TLF.

✦

That experience became more than just a chapter of his childhood. It became a lesson he carried with him: sometimes, all a child needs is an opportunity and someone willing to believe in them.

Years later, Roshan Sir chose to pass that opportunity forward.

He established two learning centres in his area where he teaches children completely free of cost. He particularly supports children from disadvantaged communities, including those living in settlements beside canals.

For these children, his centres are not merely classrooms. They are spaces where a child can learn, grow, dream, and imagine a future beyond their surroundings.

“

There is something beautiful about Roshan Sir’s journey. Once, he was a student receiving support. Today, he is the one extending that support to others.

The helping hand that once reached him has now become his own hand, reaching out to the next child.

SUCCESS STORY 02
Puja Mistri’s
02

Some flowers bloom when the conditions are perfect. Others bloom because, despite everything around them, they keep reaching for the light.

Puja Mistri’s journey is one such story.

Puja comes from Jharkhali village in the Sundarbans and grew up in a financially disadvantaged family. Despite the circumstances around her, she continued to pursue her education. She completed her Higher Secondary education from a government school and later pursued her higher education at Sree Sree Ramakrishna B.Ed. College.

After completing her studies, Puja came to Kolkata with her parents. But life in the city was far from easy. The family lived in a garage in Salt Lake, while her mother worked as a domestic helper to support the family.

Yet, through all of this, Puja carried a dream quietly within her: she wanted to pursue an MBA.

She had the ambition. She had the determination. What she did not have was the financial means to take the next step.

Then came a moment that changed the direction of her journey.

✦

Puja met Shipra Ma’am, who asked her whether she wanted to pursue an MBA. Puja shared that it had always been her dream, but financial difficulties had prevented her from pursuing it.

She came from a place where dreams often have to wait for better days, carrying with her the quiet hope of pursuing an MBA. When financial hardships stood between her and her dream, Shipra Ma’am extended her hand towards her like a mother figure and guided her towards TLF, her own branch, which she opened for children who have potential but lack the financial means to pursue their dreams.

From that moment, Puja’s dream began moving towards a brighter future. TLF stepped forward, not simply as a source of financial support, but as a family that chose to believe in her future.

“

Today, TLF is sponsoring Puja’s MBA course fees, and she is pursuing her MBA at EIILM-Kolkata.

Her journey is still unfolding. She is still learning, still growing, and still moving towards the future she once thought might remain only a dream.

Like a flower that is still blooming, Puja’s story is not about reaching the end. It is about finally being given the light, care, and opportunity to grow.

And perhaps that is what TLF’s success truly looks like: not simply changing where a person is today, but helping them discover where they can go tomorrow.
