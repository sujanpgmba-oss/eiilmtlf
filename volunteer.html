What We Do Across Our Branches

Free Education

Providing regular tuition, free books, notebooks, and essential study materials to underprivileged children.

Nutritional Food

Distributing healthy, hygienic, and nutritious meals regularly to ensure child health and growth.

Computer Education

Teaching basic computer skills and digital literacy to empower kids for a tech-driven future.

Mental Counselling

Offering mental wellness support, psychological guidance, and personal care for emotional growth.

Puja New Garments

Spreading festive smiles by gifting brand new clothes to every child during Durga Puja.

Winter Garments

Distributing warm sweaters, jackets, and blankets to shield children from harsh winter weather.
