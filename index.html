ABOUT TOWARDS LIFE FOUNDATION

Introduction of TLF

Towards Life Foundation (TLF) was established in 2006 by Prof. (Dr.) R. P. Banerjee, Chairman & Director of EIILM-KOLKATA, during the construction of South City Mall in Kolkata. Deeply moved by the hardship and suffering of the displaced construction workers and surrounding slum communities, he felt a strong calling to extend meaningful support and restore hope in their lives.

To address these human challenges, TLF was created as a dedicated Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiative. Its foundational philosophy is rooted in "Prana Dipti" — The Glowing Vitals, which symbolizes awakening inner vitality, hope, and dignity.

History of TLF

The journey of Towards Life Foundation (TLF) began in 2006 under the visionary leadership of Prof. (Dr.) R. P. Banerjee, Chairman & Director of EIILM-Kolkata, after observing the hardships of displaced construction workers and slum families during the construction of South City Mall. Established to provide basic education and human development resources to deprived children, TLF conducted a comprehensive research survey across Kolkata in 2006 to understand local social, educational, and health challenges, laying the foundation for a sustainable intervention model.

Our Handmade Shop POWERED BY EIILM-KOLKATA & TOWARDS LIFE FOUNDATION

Every piece in our collection—including tote bags, pouch bags, scented candles, and handmade jewelry—is meticulously designed and hand-painted by the UG & PG students of EIILM-Kolkata. All necessary raw materials are fully sponsored by EIILM-Kolkata.

100% of the revenue generated from these sales goes directly towards supporting the children across all TLF centers. This fund is utilized for emergency assistance, branch events, providing winter garments, and purchasing new festive dresses during Durga Puja. Furthermore, we regularly organize handicraft skill-development workshops in all our branches so these children can learn creative crafts alongside their formal education.
