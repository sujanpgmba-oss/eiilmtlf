Briefing

“Towards Life Foundation(TLF)” has embarked on its voyage of providing Education, Economic Independence and Health & Hygiene Development to the slum children residing in length and breadth of Kolkata with a mission to seek Social Inclusiveness for them.

Transforming Future Leaders

Our Cultural Vision

Cultural & Art Work

TLF Helps believes that education extends beyond academic learning. Cultural participation, creative expression, and artistic activities play an important role in nurturing confidence, imagination, communication skills, and individuality.

Through cultural programmes, creative workshops, competitions, and recreational activities, the Foundation encourages children to explore their talents, express their ideas, and discover their creative potential.

Key Initiatives

What We Do

i. Showcasing Talent through Performance

Children showcase their talents in dance, music, recitation, drama, and other performing arts during annual celebrations and cultural programmes.

ii. Inspiring Creativity through Art

Art and drawing competitions encourage children to express their ideas while enhancing creativity, observation, and artistic skills.

iii. Learning through Handicrafts & Clay Art

Creative sessions introduce children to tote bags, pouches, clay jewellery, and other handmade crafts, building both practical and artistic skills.

iv. Creativity in Action for Social Good

Students' handmade creations are featured through "Our Shop" on the organization's website, connecting creativity with social impact and generating resources for education, healthcare, and well-being.
