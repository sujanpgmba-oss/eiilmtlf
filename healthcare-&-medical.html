About This Initiative

Healthcare is the Foundation of a Thriving Community

Recognizing that meaningful societal development extends far beyond classroom schooling, TLF focuses equally on primary healthcare, adequate childhood nutrition, safe drinking water, preventive awareness, and mental resilience.

OUR CORE PURPOSE

Supporting Health, Dignity & Well-Being

TLF’s healthcare team brings essential check-ups and medical awareness directly to slums and informal settlements across Kolkata and surrounding districts.

The focus remains on early diagnosis, instilling daily hygiene habits, and connecting families with hospital resources whenever specialized medical intervention is necessary.

OUR APPROACH

Care That Connects Health With Dignity

TLF follows a disciplined cycle: understand the local community's medical shortcomings, foster preventive habits, diagnose issues early, and secure institutional medical care.

Holistic Well-Being

More Than Medical Care

Healthcare delivers its strongest impact when tied directly to clean nutrition, potable drinking water, and safe environments.

PILLAR 01

Nutrition

Providing nutrient-dense mid-day meals and fortified snacks to combat developmental malnutrition.

PILLAR 02

Safe Water

Supplying clean drinking water storage facilities and filtering solutions to eliminate gastrointestinal infections.

PILLAR 03

Hygiene Awareness

Simple, persistent education on handwashing, dental care, and seasonal dengue/malaria prevention.

PILLAR 04

Creative Health

Fostering mental equilibrium, stress release, and emotional confidence through yoga and guided art workshops.
