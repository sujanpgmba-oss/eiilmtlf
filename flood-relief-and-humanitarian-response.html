Humanitarian Response

Standing Strong Beyond the Classroom

TLF extends its social commitment beyond regular educational and community programmes by responding to communities affected by natural disasters and humanitarian emergencies.

NORTH BENGAL FLOODS 2025

Action in Times of Natural Calamity

During the severe North Bengal floods in 2025, TLF undertook relief and humanitarian assistance initiatives to support affected communities.

Action Strategy

Key Initiatives

Extending active social responsibility and community resilience through organized emergency response.

01
🙋‍♂️

Student Volunteer Participation

Students associated with the TLF Jalpaiguri branch actively participated in local relief efforts from the early stages of the disaster. Their involvement reflected the Foundation's emphasis on developing social responsibility and community participation among young learners.

02
🚛

On-Ground Support from EIILM-Kolkata

The core TLF team, along with student volunteers from EIILM-Kolkata, travelled to affected areas to provide direct assistance and distribute essential relief materials among impacted communities.

03
📦

Community-Based Relief Collaboration

Children and volunteers associated with the Jalpaiguri branch worked alongside the relief team in preparing supply kits and assisting with the distribution of food, essential supplies, and emergency materials to flood-affected families.

"This response demonstrated TLF's broader commitment to community resilience, humanitarian assistance, and active social responsibility, extending its work beyond the classroom and into communities during times of crisis."
